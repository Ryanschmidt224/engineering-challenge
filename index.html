Project 001

Launch a foam dart 50 feet.

Design, build, and test a mechanism that reliably launches a foam dart at least 50 feet. Choose your own solution and how far you want to investigate each engineering question.

The goal

Launch a foam dart to a measured horizontal distance of at least 50 feet, safely and repeatably.

Demonstrate your result with a consistent setup and recorded measurements.

What you decide

Energy source, launch mechanism, motor or actuator, power system, control method, construction, and measurement process.

There is no prescribed path or required complexity.

Possible starting questions

How fast does the dart need to go?

Explore speed, launch angle, drag, and real-world measurement.

How should the mechanism move it?

Compare flywheels, springs, compressed air, and electromagnetic concepts.

How do I power and control it?

Learn about motors, ESCs, power supplies, and safe testing.

Safety first: Use foam darts only, wear eye protection, keep clear of moving wheels and electrical contacts, and never aim at people or animals. Disconnect power before modifying wiring. A launch-distance target is not a reason to increase projectile hardness or use dangerous pressures.

Example: a converter failure is a learning path

Observation

A DC-DC converter fails during power-up, even when the input polarity appears correct.

Questions

Was the input voltage within rating? Was there a startup transient, inrush current, wiring problem, defective module, or an inductive load?

Evidence to collect

Supply model, module specifications, wiring diagram, input voltage waveform, current measurements, and photos of the failed component.

Understanding

Separate confirmed measurements from possible explanations. Do not label a hypothesis as the cause until the evidence supports it.
